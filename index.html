Royal Family news in 60 seconds, every day.

Windsor Daily is a personal, non-commercial tool built by an independent creator. Every day, it reads public news coverage about the British Royal Family and turns the main stories into short vertical videos, published on the creator's own YouTube channel: Windsor Daily.

Watch on YouTube

Videos are published on YouTube with the YouTube API Services.

How it works

  1. Collect — once a day, public news headlines and articles about the Royal Family are read from public news feeds, and the most covered stories of the day are selected.
  2. Generate — a desktop script writes a short summary of each story, with its sources, and renders a 1080×1920 video with a synthetic voice, original graphics and freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons.
  3. Publish — the videos are uploaded to the creator's own channel with the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), scheduled for later the same day, added to the channel's own playlists and given one comment from the channel.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The tool runs on the owner's computer and is signed in to a single YouTube channel through Google's official OAuth sign-in. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts and does not access anyone else's channel or data.

Data and privacy

Windsor Daily only manages videos, playlists and comments on the owner's own channel and reads that channel's name to confirm the connection. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Details are in the Privacy Policy. Use of the tool is subject to the Terms of Service and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Windsor Daily is an unofficial news channel. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by the Royal Household, any member of the Royal Family, YouTube or Google. Sources are credited in each video description. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licenses, credited in each video.
